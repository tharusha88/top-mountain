Sixteen places within a day trip of the resort, from 13 km to 51 km away.

13 km
Viewpoint

Idalgashinna Rock

Tall pines, drifting mist and a long view off the edge of the hill country.

13 km
Heritage

Adisham Bungalow

A 1930s Tudor-style stone house near Haputale, now a Benedictine monastery with English-style gardens.

16 km
Tea

Ella Organic Tea Factory

Walk the tea rows, see how the leaf is made, and finish with a fresh cup of Uva tea.

21 km
Adventure

Flying Ravana

A long zipline ride across a forested valley just outside Ella.

21 km
Legend

Ravana Cave

A cliffside cave near Ella, tied by legend to King Ravana of the Ramayana. Reached by a steep stairway.

21 km
Temple

Mahamevnawa Meditation Monastery

A quiet meditation monastery and a white stupa on a ridge, often wrapped in morning mist.

22 km
Railway

Nine Arch Bridge

The colonial-era railway viaduct near Ella. Time it right and watch a train curve across its nine arches.

22 km
Hike

Ella Rock

A half-day hike along the railway and through tea estates to a summit high above the Ella Gap.

22 km
Hike

Little Adam’s Peak

An easy climb from Ella through tea gardens, with wide views of Ella Rock and the gap.

25 km
Waterfall

Ravana Falls

A wide, tiered waterfall right beside the Ella–Wellawaya road.

25 km
Viewpoint

Lipton’s Seat

The lookout above Dambatenne where Sir Thomas Lipton surveyed his tea estates. Best at sunrise.

26 km
Trail

The Devil’s Staircase

A steep, hairpinned track dropping off the high plateau, loved by hikers and 4×4 drivers.

28 km
Sunrise hike

Wangedigala Rock

An early climb to a rocky top for sunrise, often above a sea of cloud.

32 km
Viewpoint

Pilkington View Point

A grassy hilltop with a lone tree, where the cloud often sits right at your feet.

42 km
Waterfall

Diyaluma Falls

Sri Lanka’s second-highest waterfall, with natural rock pools to swim in at the top.

51 km
Scenic road

Bakers Bend

A road that loops through steep green hills. Worth the drive for the bends alone.
